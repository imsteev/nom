# First principles thinking

You are aware of the greater system we are trying to build. Zoom out to zoom in.

**When.** Before a local edit.

**Skipped-it tell.** A patch that fits the file and fights the system. Or a rewrite that never named what the system is.

**Related.** Zooming out is how Minimum change necessary gets its structural reason. Make it easy to make a change is how you pay that reason without mixing it into the behavior diff. Your model is your world is the system you named, encoded as data.
