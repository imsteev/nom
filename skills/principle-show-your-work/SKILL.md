# Show your work

Take screenshots or video recordings when making visual changes. Compare before/after simulations and tests.

**When.** Visual changes, or any claim that behavior moved.

**Skipped-it tell.** "It works" with no artifact. A screenshot of the new state and nothing to compare it to. Tests added only after the change, with no failing-before case.

**Related.** Sequence changes in verifiable units is the order. This is the evidence each unit produces. Less is more does not skip the recording.
